Delete Your Data

Greedy Capitalist · MACA Studio
Effective 17 August 2026

This page explains how to delete the data that Greedy Capitalist, published by MACA Studio, stores about you. There are two ways to do it: from inside the game, or by contacting us.

Option 1 — Delete from inside the game

This removes everything immediately and does not require contacting anyone.

  1. Open Greedy Capitalist.
  2. On the title screen, tap Settings.
  3. Scroll to Clear Local Data.
  4. Confirm when prompted.
This is permanent. Clearing local data removes your anonymous player identifier. Once it is gone, your existing leaderboard entry can no longer be reached or recovered, and the game will create a new anonymous identity the next time it connects.

If you only want to reset your statistics without losing your identity, use Reset Statistics in the same Settings screen instead. That clears your local stats and achievements while leaving your leaderboard best results in place.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

To have data associated with your player identifier removed from our servers, email aldo.minano@gmail.com with the subject line Data deletion request.

Include your in-game display name so we can locate the record. Requests are processed within 30 days.

What gets deleted, and what is kept

Data Result
Anonymous player identifier Deleted
Display name Deleted
Gameplay statistics and achievements Deleted, both locally and on our servers
Leaderboard entry Deleted
Local game settings Deleted, restored to defaults
Online match data Not retained. Match state exists only while a game is in progress and is cleared when the match ends. Nothing is kept afterward.

We hold no additional retention copies. Once data is deleted it is not recoverable, and no residual record is kept beyond what Google's Firebase infrastructure may retain in routine backups, which are governed by the Firebase Privacy and Security documentation.
