Privacy Policy

Greedy Capitalist · MACA Studio
Effective 17 August 2026

This policy explains what information Greedy Capitalist collects, why it is collected, and how you can remove it. It applies to the Android, web, and desktop versions of the game.

Information we collect

Greedy Capitalist does not ask for or collect personally identifying information. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, physical address, contacts, photos, or location.

The game does collect the following:

  • An anonymous player identifier. When you first launch the game, it creates an anonymous account through Firebase Authentication. This produces a random identifier that is not linked to your real identity, your Google account, or any personal information. It exists so that your statistics and leaderboard position can be attributed to you across sessions.
  • A display name. Whatever name you choose to enter in the game. This is self-selected and does not need to be your real name.
  • Gameplay statistics. Games played, wins and losses, fastest win, highest portfolio value, cards played, and achievements earned.
  • Leaderboard entries. Your display name and best results, which are visible to other players in the in-game leaderboard.
  • Online match data. If you play an online multiplayer game, the game temporarily stores match state — the room code, moves made, and player names — so both players stay in sync. This data is readable only by the two participants in that match.

What we do not do

  • We do not show advertising.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking services.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or profiling of any kind.

Where your data is stored

Gameplay statistics are stored locally on your device. If you are connected to the internet, they are also synchronised to Firebase Realtime Database, a service operated by Google. Google's handling of that data is governed by the Firebase Privacy and Security documentation and the Google Privacy Policy.

Solo games against the computer opponent work entirely offline and do not require a network connection.

Deleting your data

The game includes two options under Settings:

  • Reset Statistics clears your local statistics and achievements. Your leaderboard best results are preserved.
  • Clear Local Data removes all locally stored data, including your statistics, your settings, and your anonymous player identifier. This is permanent. Once your identifier is removed, your existing leaderboard position can no longer be recovered, and the game will create a new anonymous identity the next time it connects.

If you would like data associated with your player identifier removed from our servers, contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

Children

Greedy Capitalist is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Because the game collects no personally identifying information from anyone, it holds no data that could identify a child. If you believe a child has provided information that should be removed, please contact us.
